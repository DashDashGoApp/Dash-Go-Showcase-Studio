DASH-GO SHOWCASE STUDIO

Explore Dash-Go with a safe, disposable household.

This runs the real Dash-Go dashboard locally with fictional sample data. It never touches a real Dash-Go installation, calendar, account, or browser profile.

What you can explore

  • Calendar, weather, agenda, and rotating household messages
  • Grocery lists, chores, routines, and Family Message Board
  • App Launcher, Dashboard Control, themes, and display settings

The city shapes sample events, map position, household wording, and local calendar dates. All data resets when Studio closes.
