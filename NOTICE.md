Dash-Go Showcase Studio is a separate, disposable demonstration product.

It includes a pinned Dash-Go 1.5.2 source baseline solely to build the local
Showcase runtime. It does not modify the published Dash-Go 1.5.2 appliance
release, and it must never read, import, or overwrite a real Dash-Go install.
